# Heatwave Action Plan
Document ID: HEAT-ACTION-2025

## HEAT-1: Heat Alert Meaning
Yellow indicates hot conditions requiring public awareness. Orange indicates severe heat requiring reduced outdoor activity. Red indicates extreme heat requiring active protection for vulnerable groups.

## HEAT-2: Vulnerable Groups
Priority groups include children, elderly persons, outdoor workers, pregnant women, persons with disabilities, people living in tin-roof homes, and people without reliable cooling access.

## HEAT-3: Immediate Actions During Orange or Red Alert
District teams should open cooling centres, arrange drinking water points, adjust school outdoor activities, notify employers of outdoor workers, and run public awareness messages.

## HEAT-4: Health System Preparedness
Primary health centres should stock ORS, maintain heatstroke treatment protocols, ensure ambulance readiness, and track heat illness cases daily.

## HEAT-5: Cool Roof and Shade Measures
Cool roof coating, temporary shade, tree canopy, water stations, and ventilation improvements can reduce local heat stress.
