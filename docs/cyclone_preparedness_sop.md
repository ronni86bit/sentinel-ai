# Cyclone Preparedness SOP
Document ID: CYCLONE-SOP-2025

## CYC-1: Preparedness Timeline
At 72 hours, verify shelters and communication lines. At 48 hours, warn coastal and low-lying communities. At 24 hours, move vulnerable groups and secure loose materials.

## CYC-2: Shelter Readiness
Shelters must have drinking water, toilets, first-aid kits, lighting, phone-charging arrangements, and space for women, children, elderly people, and persons with disabilities.

## CYC-3: Fisherfolk Advisory
When a cyclone warning is active, fishing boats must not venture into sea. Harbour officers should confirm boat return status and communicate advisories in local language.

## CYC-4: Critical Infrastructure
Power substations, hospitals, telecom towers, ports, and water-supply assets should be checked for wind, surge, and backup power exposure.

## CYC-5: Damage Reporting
Initial damage reports should separate life safety issues, road blockage, power failure, building damage, and water contamination.
