# District Incident Response Protocol
Document ID: DIST-IRP-2025

## IRP-1: Control Room Activation
The district control room should be activated when any Orange or Red alert is issued, or when more than three blocks report disruption.

## IRP-2: Common Operating Picture
All departments should update a shared incident board with location, hazard, severity, population affected, blocked routes, available teams, and unmet needs.

## IRP-3: Resource Prioritization
Priority should consider severity, vulnerable population, time since last contact, medical need, accessibility, and availability of safe shelter.

## IRP-4: Interdepartmental Coordination
Revenue, police, fire, health, public works, electricity, water supply, and local bodies must nominate nodal officers for incident coordination.

## IRP-5: Decision Logs
Major decisions must be logged with timestamp, decision owner, reason, affected location, and follow-up action.
