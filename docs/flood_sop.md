# District Flood Response SOP
Document ID: FLOOD-SOP-2025

## FLOOD-1: Alert Levels
Green means monitoring only. Yellow means prepare ward teams and verify pumps. Orange means activate control room and pre-position rescue assets. Red means initiate evacuation from low-lying zones and open relief shelters.

## FLOOD-2: Pre-Monsoon Preparedness
Before monsoon, the district must clean storm-water drains, inspect culverts, test dewatering pumps, mark low-lying settlements, and update contact lists for ward officers.

## FLOOD-3: Evacuation Triggers
Evacuation should be considered when water enters homes, when river gauge crosses danger level, when two consecutive high-intensity rainfall alerts are received, or when road access is likely to be cut off.

## FLOOD-4: Public Communication
Messages must mention expected location, timing, safe routes, shelter locations, and helpline numbers. Avoid technical terms that citizens cannot understand.

## FLOOD-5: Post-Event Assessment
After water recedes, teams must record affected households, infrastructure damage, water contamination, health risks, and urgent repair needs.
