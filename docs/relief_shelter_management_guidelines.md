# Relief Shelter Management Guidelines
Document ID: SHELTER-GUIDE-2025

## SHELTER-1: Minimum Services
Every shelter must provide safe drinking water, toilets, lighting, sleeping space, basic first aid, and information about family tracing.

## SHELTER-2: Registration
Shelter managers must register every household entering the shelter and record special needs such as medicines, disability support, pregnancy, and unaccompanied children.

## SHELTER-3: Safety and Inclusion
Separate spaces should be provided for women, children, elderly persons, and persons with disabilities where possible. Complaint mechanisms should be visible.

## SHELTER-4: Health and Hygiene
Daily cleaning, waste disposal, vector control, safe food distribution, and health screening should be maintained.

## SHELTER-5: Closure
A shelter should close only after return routes are safe, homes are assessed, and minimum services are restored.
